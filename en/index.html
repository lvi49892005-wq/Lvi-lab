Welcome to the Lab of Viral Immunology at The Catholic University of Korea.

We develop prophylactic vaccines and therapeutics based on mRNA technology. Working with partner institutions, we improve the mRNA platform and screen new lipid nanoparticle (LNP) carriers to build more effective mRNA vaccines.

About

Building on virology, vaccinology, and immunology, our laboratory researches and develops vaccines for preventing infectious diseases and therapeutics for treating disease. Our work began with vaccine vectors based on Japanese encephalitis virus and coxsackievirus. Today, with mRNA as our core platform, we are expanding into infectious disease vaccines, cancer vaccines, and mRNA therapeutics that can replace conventional protein drugs.

The efficacy and safety of an mRNA medicine are determined by the structural design of the mRNA itself and by the lipid nanoparticle (LNP) technology that delivers it into the body. We have therefore studied mRNA structure optimization, including the UTRs and poly(A) tail, as well as the control of impurities such as dsRNA and quality assessment. We are also developing safer and more effective vaccines through the design and screening of new ionizable and helper lipids. In parallel, we pursue LNP targeting for precise delivery to specific cells or organs, together with nonclinical toxicity studies, to build an integrated platform with proven efficacy and safety.

Research of this kind cannot be completed by one laboratory alone. Through close collaboration with universities, research institutes, and companies in Korea and abroad, we are committed to turning basic research findings into vaccines and therapeutics in practical use.

We welcome the interest and participation of researchers and partner institutions who wish to join us in research that contributes to human health.

Principal Investigator: Prof. Jae-Hwan Nam, Department of Medical and Biological Sciences.

Research

01

Next-Generation mRNA Design Platform

Precise design of mRNA structure, including the 5′ UTR and poly(A) tail, and impurity control for higher expression and stability.

02

Precision LNP Delivery System

Design and screening of new ionizable and helper lipids for safer lipid nanoparticles that reach target tissues selectively.

03

From Prevention to Therapeutics

Extending mRNA technology from infectious disease vaccines to cancer vaccines and protein replacement therapeutics for intractable diseases.
